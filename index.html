One instinct, three arenas

Work from the whole down to the parts — applied to products, direction, and organizations.

Products

The Converger

I take products that have grown apart and merge them into one coherent experience — mapping users' mental models and turning their own groupings into the architecture.

Proof: MB5 shipped 2023 as one unified app.
✧
Direction

The Strategist

I start from vision and work down — grounded in evidence, not opinion. Most strategy work is deciding what not to do, and I can design the study that tests it.

Proof: One study redirected a mobile-first strategy.

The System-builder

I build the ops that let research scale and be heard — intake to planning to broadcast — so findings reach people every cycle, with or without me in the room.

Proof: Grew a 1-person function into a team of 4.
