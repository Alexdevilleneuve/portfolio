the through-line

“Research, I learned, is as much an art as a science.”

Before I studied people, I spent seven years studying mice — neuropeptides and cellular pathways — in a world where enough rigor would eventually reveal a single root cause.

Humans, it turns out, are considerably less cooperative. That complication attracted me: interpretation, judgment, and empathy matter just as much as methodological rigor.

At Google REACH-UX and Malwarebytes (Senior UXR → Research Manager), my work moved from evaluating experiences to shaping the systems behind them — including research that informed a $100M portfolio expansion.

03 how I think

How I think about the work.

Understand the system → explain the mechanism → influence direction early → make the evidence travel.

The feature is rarely the whole problem.

I look for the mental model underneath.

What happened isn’t enough. I want to know why.

I trace findings until the explanation survives scrutiny.

Research as the compass, not the brake.

I invest upstream, while direction is still cheap to change.

A finding that doesn’t travel didn’t happen.

Evidence creates value when it reaches the decisions it should change.
