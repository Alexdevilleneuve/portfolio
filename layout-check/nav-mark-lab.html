The nav becomes a mark

The pill still hides on the way down. What stays is a small mark that turns with the scroll, and a click brings the bar back. On 04, the bar becomes the mark only while leaving the hero, then the mark stays in the top left. Scroll up and the bar returns beside it. The ring of type turns clockwise on the way down, and back on the way up. Scroll inside a frame.

02

Crown of the line

The pill leaves. The compass comes to rest above the chapter line and keeps turning. Click it to bring the bar back.

03

Sailboat

The pill shrinks into a glass coin, with the little boat. It heels as you travel, and it rests when the page rests.

04

Shrink, then roll left

The bar shrinks from both sides into a coin, then rolls to the top left. That plays once, on the way down from the hero. After that the mark stays put. Scroll up and the bar returns beside it.
